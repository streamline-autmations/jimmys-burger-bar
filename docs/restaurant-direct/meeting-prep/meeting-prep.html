Jimmy's Burger Bar: meeting prep

2026-11-19 Internal, do not hand to the owner

Goal. Show how Restaurant Direct works and find out whether Jimmy's wants to pilot collection first and test delivery with its own driver.

Open with. I'd like Jimmy's to have its own link for collection and delivery with your own driver. Customers can order and pay in a few taps. The ticket lands on the till computer and the chef's phone, and customers can use the same link every time.

The two-minute demo

Have ready: do the 10-minute checklist in DEMO-SCRIPT.md (trading hours, sound on both devices, old tickets cleared, app installed with one collected order).

  1. The app. Open Jimmy's app from the Home Screen. It starts on the menu.
  2. Order and pay. Add a Beef Burger, pay with the Payfast sandbox, tap Tell me when it is ready.
  3. Kitchen. It chimes under To accept on both screens, only after Payfast confirms.
  4. Accept and ready. Promise a time, Mark ready: the guest phone shows Ready to collect.
  5. Reorder. My orders, then Order again: the cart refills at today's prices. Don't place it.
  6. Month end. Report with CSV, then Ordering: pause and sold out. Only if staging has been redeployed; otherwise say it is "built and coming with the next update".

Pricing options to discuss

Current build plan proposal. R0 upfront, then R999/month or 15% of completed direct food sales, whichever is higher, with a 90-day review. Jimmy's pays Payfast through its own account and handles fulfilment and refunds. Start with collection. Agree delivery scope and pricing separately after testing.

Alternative (your call). A lower minimum, so the deal is clearly cheaper than Mr D even in a slow month.

What Jimmy's pays Payfast (its own account, ex VAT, no monthly fee): Instant EFT and Capitec Pay 2% (min R2), card, Apple Pay and Google Pay 3.2% + R2, SnapScan 3.5% + R2, R8.70 per payout, R2 per refund.

Still to agree: whether the 15% is on food with or without VAT, whether Streamline adds VAT, how refunds net off, and that "month" means calendar month.

Questions to ask the owner

  • Collection volume. How many phone or WhatsApp collection orders did you take in the last 30 days, and what were the food sales?
  • Staff. Who accepts orders and handles busy periods? Who can refund? Which payment method will you select in the POS?
  • Equipment. Can the POS PC run a browser alongside the till? What phone does the chef use? Who needs a login, and who is the backup?
  • Launch. What are the current menu, till prices, hours and kitchen capacity? How will you show the link on Google, Instagram and in-store?
  • Driver. What hours does your driver work, and are they available on Fridays? How are they paid per trip? How far can the scooter go? Who takes cash or card at the door, if anyone?
  • Terms. Which pilot price would you approve? How will we define the commission base, including food VAT, and reconcile completed sales?

Likely objections

We're already on Mr D.
Compare a real basket using your Mr D statement.
Customers just WhatsApp us.
Share the link on WhatsApp so regulars can order and pay directly.
What if the internet is down?
Retry when data returns. Use WhatsApp if reachable.
What does it cost me?
Nothing upfront. R999 a month or 15% of direct food sales, whichever is higher. At R15k that is R2,250, plus about R345 Payfast by EFT.

Don't say

  • Skip sales and results claims. Vendor case-study percentages are not promises.
  • Call it a staging demo with a Payfast sandbox payment. The MVP is unfinished. Don't say "customise": there are no sauce or side options yet. Don't quote a "commission kept" figure: no Mr D rate is set. The ready alert isn't proven on a real phone yet, so rehearse it on yours. Rehearse checkout too: the kitchen-push line had a Payfast checkout bug (TaskHub 62bb1711).
  • Keep R39/R49 provisional. Ask which delivery zones to cover and what each trip costs before agreeing fees.
