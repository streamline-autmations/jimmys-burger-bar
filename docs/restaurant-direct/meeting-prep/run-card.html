Jimmy's: demo run card

Sat 21 Nov. Live site (they scroll), then the demo (you drive), then the money.

Before you walk in

  • Window A: the live site, opened fresh just before (so they see the intro).
  • Window B: the demo, zoom 75%. Tab 1 /admin/kitchen (tap Enable sound). Tab 2 /order: Controls, Reset demo records, Show as installed app.
  • Phone charged, Do Not Disturb on, screen timeout off. Wifi and phone data both tested.
  • Prep PDF open in another window. For your eyes only, never on the shared screen.

1. Jimmy's live site 3 to 5 min, their pace

jimmys-burger-bar.vercel.app. Hand it over and let them scroll. Don't narrate.

Never place an order here. It is the real site: it writes to Jimmy's real database and emails the real inbox. If they want to try ordering, give them the demo.

Then: "Now let me show you what you'd see on your side when a customer orders."

2. The demo 2 min, your pace

restaurant-direct-demo.vercel.app

  1. Menu opens on Breakfast. Tap Burgers.
  2. Add Smash Burger and Beef Burger.
  3. View cart, then Checkout.
  4. Controls, Fill guest details on this page, Place order.
  5. Switch to the kitchen. Scroll or zoom: the new order sits at the bottom of To accept.
  6. Tap a time, Start preparing, Mark ready.
  7. Guest side: Track this order, Tell me when it is ready. The alert is simulated.
  8. Kitchen: Collected. Guest: My orders, Order again.
  9. Controls, Order report, Download CSV.
  10. Controls, The week in numbers. "That's commission you keep."

Lost? Controls, Jump to. Wifi dead? Tether your phone.

3. Money and questions use the prep PDF

Ask first

  • Their Mr D statement (rate, fees, payouts).
  • Phone or WhatsApp collection orders in the last 30 days.
  • Who accepts orders? Does the till PC run a browser?
  • Driver hours? Fridays?

Don't say

  • "Customise" (not on Jimmy's yet; don't tap it).
  • Any "commission kept" figure as theirs. 25% is a placeholder.
  • That the ready alert works on every phone. Say "simulated".
  • Delivery prices as agreed. R39/R49 is provisional.
  • If asked about food photos: many are AI stand-ins until a real shoot.
